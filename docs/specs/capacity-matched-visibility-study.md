# Capacity-matched visibility study

Status: revised prospective analysis design; the ten-replicate balanced pilot is exploratory, and the [held-out balanced-landscape payoff gate](../reviews/balanced-capacity-payoff-gate-2026-10-01.md) supports all six scenario/return cells. The independent full learning campaign has not run. The earlier [Stage-5 study](visibility-bounded-search-study.md) and its results remain historical.

## Research questions and hypotheses

The social-observation treatment remains Stage-5: five initial visibility profiles, fixed versus adaptive bounded attention, plus a B0 ecological baseline. The new ecology factor isolates spatial resource organization while holding total capacity fixed.

- **RQ1:** How do capacity-matched resource layouts, initial visibility profiles, and adaptive observation affect how accurately agents' four observed peers represent population extraction behavior?
- **RQ2:** How do those conditions affect collective extraction, inequality in actual observer counts, resource persistence, reserve welfare, and wealth inequality?
- **H1, realized visibility:** Across training, greater inequality in actual observer counts is associated with greater local-view error. The primary longitudinal estimate centers visibility Gini and mean absolute local-view error within each adaptive run, then pools matched training checkpoints across the three landscapes and five initial profiles. Resample independent replicate IDs as clusters across the treatment grid for a 95% correlation interval. A second estimate removes each run's linear time trend before calculating the correlation; a separate between-run analysis compares full-training averages after centering within treatment. These are associations, not identified causal effects of observer counts. Fixed networks have no within-run visibility change and appear in the between-run analysis only.
- **H2, spatial segregation:** Averaged equally over five initial profiles and both network dynamics, segregated resource layout produces greater final-window local-view error than dispersed layout. For each replicate, pair segregated−dispersed within each of the ten social-treatment cells, then average those ten differences. The primary directional prediction is a positive mean contrast. Both landscapes hold total capacity and the multiset of local capacities fixed. Report all ten cell contrasts and the two uniform comparisons as heterogeneity/context; they are not ten independent confirmatory tests.
- **H3, adaptive observation:** Averaged equally over three landscapes and five initial profiles, adaptive bounded rewiring reduces final-window local-view error relative to fixed observation starting from the same graph. For each replicate, average the fifteen paired adaptive−fixed differences. The primary directional prediction is a negative mean contrast. Report all fifteen cell contrasts and changes in visibility Gini and low-extraction share as companion results.

Local-view error is the absolute difference between the share of low extraction among an agent's four observed peers and that among all other agents, averaged across agents. It measures the representativeness of information available to an agent, not an elicited belief. This revised analysis contract was specified after the exploratory balanced pilot existed; none of its hypotheses can be confirmed with that pilot. The independent held-out full campaign is the prospective test. No hypothesis asserts that more resource segregation necessarily reduces welfare; resource dynamics and fixed agent positions make that sign uncertain.

## Frozen landscape contract

The new scenario IDs are `balanced_uniform`, `balanced_dispersed`, and `balanced_segregated`. On a 10×10 grid, each has total K=75. Uniform has K=0.75 in all cells. The two unequal maps each have fifty K=0.55 and fifty K=0.95 cells; the dispersed map uses the landscape seed to permute positions, and the segregated map arranges low and high capacity by column. The unequal maps have the same capacity histogram in every replicate. All maps use regeneration r=0.05, equilibrium fraction q=0.70, initial resource 0.5K, and neighbor coupling c=0.10. Landscape seed is campaign seed plus replicate; other paired seeds and all social parameters follow the earlier Stage-5 study.

The new `environment_design=balanced_capacity_v1` identity is stored in every run configuration and campaign freeze. Analyses reject mixed environment designs and differing scenario sets. Historical scenario definitions and reference hashes remain untouched.

## Validation gates and measurements

1. **Structural audit:** verify total K within numerical tolerance, identical unequal-map capacity multisets, constant r/q, and more unlike-neighbor edges in the dispersed map. Record per-replicate map SHA-256 values and no-harvest stock at steps 100 and 1000 in `landscape_validation.csv`. This baseline measures any physical supply effect of spatial exchange and clipping before agents act.
2. **Commons-dilemma gate — passed:** the independent population-payoff experiment used the new scenarios, capped-harvest reward, N=64, H=1000, C=always-low, D=always-high, and 100 held-out replicates. Collective gain G>0, exploitation gap E>0, and fear>0 have positive simultaneous lower bounds for both summed and discounted utility in each ecology. All six verdicts are `supported`. The full learning runner checks the exact frozen gate configuration and all six verdicts through `scripts.validate_balanced_gate`.
3. **Learning smoke and pilot:** use paired condition seeds and the unchanged 11 treatments per ecology. Mechanical smoke has N=8 and two replicates. Figure smoke has N=64, two short replicates and renders the candidate story. The exploratory pilot uses N=64, ten replicates and the full training/evaluation lengths. The confirmatory full campaign uses 100 new replicates per ecology/treatment after the gate.

The primary aggregate sustainability metric is `total_resource / total_capacity` on fresh evaluation. `mean(R_i/K_i)` is retained as a separate mean local fill fraction. RQ2 reports final-window low-extraction share and visibility Gini, plus fresh-evaluation resource persistence, mean reserve welfare (energy reserve / maximum reserve), and final wealth Gini. Paired ecology, fixed-network profile, and adaptive−fixed tables cover these five outcomes; descriptive absolute outcomes include the B0 ecological baseline. Agent-region summaries distinguish high- and low-capacity exposure in the segregated map. Replicates, not agents or checkpoints, are the inferential units. Primary H2/H3 intervals are replicate-bootstrap 95%; H1 resamples replicate IDs across the treatment grid. Cellwise intervals are pointwise, not familywise significance tests.

## Figures and interpretation

The five story candidates show (1) the three capacity maps with a common scale and equal total K, (2) who gets seen, (3) the H2 paired ecology contrasts beside the H1 within-run checkpoint association, (4) fresh-evaluation resource persistence against reserve welfare and wealth Gini plus a separately labeled training-end regional gap, and (5) adaptive−fixed differences in visibility, local-view error and extraction. Mean local fill remains in the analysis tables for supplementary reporting. They render from the saved analysis and source runs, export PDF/SVG/PNG, and write captions plus a provenance manifest. The analysis also retains full factor grids, RQ2 outcome contrasts, and primary pooled H2/H3 estimates. These are candidate manuscript figures rather than evidence that the predictions passed.

The new design licenses a claim about **capacity-matched spatial organization**, conditional on the tested policy pair, resource dynamics, grid and agent positions. It does not guarantee equal unharvested realized stock or validate the commons dilemma until the new payoff gate passes.
