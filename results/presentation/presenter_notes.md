## Slide 2

The schematic describes the feedback, not every implementation step. Agents observe previous source actions alongside local resource/capacity, choose extraction with independent tabular Q-learning, experience resource renewal, compare expected versus observed peer actions and may replace information sources. Surprise selects observers; removed and replacement sources are random conditional on the search rule. Local replacement is sources-of-sources in the information graph, not geographical proximity. Reward is realized harvest. Stationary locations, proportional allocation under scarcity and a 3×3 state representation restrict the behavioral mechanism. The review found 97.1% scarce-state occupancy in fresh-reset evaluation, 99.7% in continuation. These are diagnostics, not causal proof of a representation failure.

## Slide 6

Figure uses outcome_summary.csv, all nine treatments and all three ecologies. Across eight social treatments, split−uniform welfare is −9.49 to −11.92 percentage points and wealth Gini +0.169 to +0.175. These are ranges of treatment-specific effects, not confidence intervals or pooled effects. Resource metric is mean local R/K, not total stock; a larger normalized fraction does not imply a richer ecology. R0 paired effects: all nine wealth-Gini intervals include zero, eight of nine welfare and eight of nine resource intervals include zero. The isolated positive intervals are unadjusted and do not establish broad benefit. The 72.3% fresh-reset versus 26.2% continuation welfare diagnostic equally weights 27 condition means. Reset restores ecology and full initial energy; it is not a pure energy intervention. Neither 1000-step evaluation establishes long-run sustainability. Existing values/paired means were independently checked against raw tables in the review.
